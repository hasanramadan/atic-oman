Our heritage

One family name.
Two homes.

Yamama started in Lebanon in 1964, founded by Ahmad Ataya. It grew into a leading maker of vinegars, grenadine molasses, flower waters and syrups, sold across the Gulf and beyond.

Ataya Trading & Industrial Co. LLC (ATIC Oman) brings the same family name, recipes and quality standards to Oman, where we produce Yamama products locally.
